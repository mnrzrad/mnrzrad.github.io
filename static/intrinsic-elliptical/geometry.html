Figure 1 · Geometry of anisotropic intrinsic elliptical distributions

Explore the Riemannian exponential map and the same quadratic form on the tangent plane and sphere. Drag the 3D spheres to rotate them; use the mouse wheel to zoom. The density surface uses the normalised log density with a labelled colour scale.

(a) Riemannian geometry

(b) Tangent-space level sets

(c) Spherical density and contour

Model: μ = (0,0,1), Λ = diag(λ₁,λ₂,0), g(s) = exp(−s/2). In (b), grey contours are isotropic reference circles (λ₁ = λ₂ = 1). In (c), the black contour is the exact exponential-map image of Q = c. The logarithm map is undefined at the antipode; the spherical colour surface excludes that single point.
