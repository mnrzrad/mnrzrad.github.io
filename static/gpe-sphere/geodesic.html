Geodesic distance on the sphere

The geodesic distance d(x, μ) = arccos(x⊤μ) is the angle θ between the two vectors at the centre O, and equals the length of the shortest arc joining them on the sphere. The Euclidean distance ‖x − μ‖ = 2 sin(θ/2) is the length of the straight segment between them, through the inside of the sphere. Drag the points to move them, drag the sphere to rotate it.
