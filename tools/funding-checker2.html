Funding Acknowledgement Checker

Find out which project grants a paper acknowledges. Upload PDFs, or upload a spreadsheet / CSV containing DOIs, and download the result as CSV.

Settings: grants to look for

One code per line; each line gets its own column. (Optional: put spellings that should share one column on the same line, separated by “|”.) Matching ignores spaces around “/”, line breaks and the doi.org form (e.g. 10.54499/UID/PRR/00297/2025).

Browsers block reading publisher websites directly, so pages are fetched through public CORS proxies, tried in order (one per line; they only see the public paper URL). In a line, {url} is replaced by the URL-encoded page address and {raw} by the plain address (otherwise the encoded address is appended). Best option: your own free Cloudflare Worker (see proxy-worker.js), e.g. https://yourname.workers.dev/?url={url}, placed on the first line. Clear the box to try direct access only. You can always click a row's website link to look yourself.
